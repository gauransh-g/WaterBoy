# India Ground Water Resource Estimation System (INGRES) and Digital Monitoring

## 1. What is INGRES?
The India Ground Water Resource Estimation System (INGRES) is an advanced, automated web-based GIS software suite co-developed by the Central Ground Water Board (CGWB) and the Indian Institute of Technology (IIT) Hyderabad. INGRES digitalizes and automates the complex mathematical algorithms and spatial data layers mandated under the GEC-2015 methodology.

## 2. Key Architectural Components of INGRES
1. **Automated Dynamic Resource Computation**: Ingests raster and vector hydrogeological layers, well monitoring networks, rainfall grids from the India Meteorological Department (IMD), specific yield values, and draft census data.
2. **Spatial Modeling and GIS Visualizations**: Generates choropleth maps indicating Stage of Ground Water Extraction (SOE), category classifications (Safe, Semi-Critical, Critical, Over-Exploited, Saline), and annual recharge distributions down to the Gram Panchayat, Block, and District tiers.
3. **Multi-Tier Analytical Dashboards**: Enables policymakers, district magistrates, hydrologists, and citizens to inspect water availability, projected industrial allocation, and domestic requirements up to the year 2025 and beyond.
4. **Data Transparency and Interoperability**: Serves as the national single source of truth for groundwater assessment, interfacing with the National Water Informatics Centre (NWIC) and the India-WRIS (Water Resources Information System) portal.

## 3. Real-Time Telemetry and Digital Water Level Recorders (DWLR)
To replace manual quarterly groundwater tape-monitoring, the Ministry of Jal Shakti has deployed a nationwide network of over 16,000 Digital Water Level Recorders (DWLRs) with satellite/GSM telemetry:
- Installed in dedicated piezometer observation wells tapping primary unconfined and semi-confined aquifers.
- Records hourly hydrostatic pressure and temperature, computing depth to water table continuously.
- Transmits telemetry packets to national cloud servers, providing real-time early warning of abrupt water table drawdown or recharge spikes following monsoon cloudbursts.

## 4. Pradhan Mantri Krishi Sinchayee Yojana (PMKSY) Synergy
INGRES assessment data provides the mandatory statutory clearance foundation for PMKSY-Per Drop More Crop micro-irrigation subsidies and PM-KUSUM solar agricultural pump installations. Under Central Ground Water Authority (CGWA) guidelines, new commercial and industrial borewell NOCs (No Objection Certificates) are strictly regulated or prohibited in blocks categorized as Over-Exploited or Critical.
