# Groundwater Wells, Pumping Machinery, Hydraulics, and Real-Time DWLR Monitoring Systems

## 1. Types of Groundwater Extraction Structures in India
India operates the largest groundwater extraction infrastructure in the world, with over 20.5 million irrigation wells according to the Minor Irrigation Census:

1. **Dug Wells (Open Wells)**:
   - **Depth**: Typically 5 to 25 meters; diameter 2 to 6 meters.
   - **Terrain**: Common in weathered hard rock zones (Maharashtra, Madhya Pradesh, Tamil Nadu, Karnataka).
   - **Characteristics**: Tap shallow unconfined phreatic aquifers. Yields range from 30 to 100 m³/day; highly prone to drying up during peak summer (March–June).

2. **Dug-cum-Bore Wells**:
   - Dug wells deepened by drilling one or more vertical or inclined small-diameter boreholes (30 to 60 m deep) through the well bottom to tap deeper joint fractures in hard rocks.

3. **Shallow Tube Wells**:
   - **Depth**: 30 to 70 meters; drilled in unconsolidated alluvial formations (Ganga-Brahmaputra plains, coastal belts).
   - Equipped with slotted PVC or stainless-steel screens opposite water-bearing sand/gravel horizons.
   - Pumping mechanism: Centrifugal pumps (for suction lifts < 7–8 m) or small submersible pumps.

4. **Deep Tube Wells / Deep Borewells**:
   - **Depth**: 100 to 400+ meters.
   - **Alluvium**: Multi-tier screened assemblies tapping confined aquifers below continuous clay aquitards.
   - **Hard Rock**: 150 mm (6-inch) diameter rotary/DTH (Down-the-Hole) hammered borewells tapping discrete fractured rock zones at 100 to 350 mbgl.
   - Pumping mechanism: Multistage electric submersible pumps or solar PV pump sets.

5. **Infiltration Galleries and Ranney Collector Wells**:
   - Horizontal perforated pipes laid beneath riverbeds in alluvial gravels, drawing riverbank-filtered groundwater with high biological purity.

## 2. Pumping Machinery and Energy Nexus
- **Submersible Pumps**: Highly efficient multistage centrifugal pumps submerged entirely in water inside the well casing. Replaced traditional surface centrifugal pumps as regional water tables dropped below the atmospheric suction limit (~7.5 meters).
- **PM-KUSUM Scheme**: Solar-powered off-grid and grid-connected agriculture pumps (up to 7.5 to 10 HP), decoupling irrigation from grid outages while requiring micro-irrigation to prevent uncontrolled solar pumping depletion.

## 3. Real-Time Telemetry: Digital Water Level Recorders (DWLR)
Under the National Hydrology Project (NHP), Central Ground Water Board and State Ground Water Departments deployed thousands of automated DWLRs across India:
- **Instrumentation**: Hydrostatic piezoresistive pressure transducers installed inside piezometer tubes, measuring instantaneous water column height above the sensor.
- **Telemetry**: GSM/GPRS cellular or satellite telemetry transmitting hourly water table depth data to the national CGWB water information portal (WIMS / INGRES).
- **Capabilities**:
  - Provides continuous hydrographs recording recharge response after individual rainfall events.
  - Detects cone of depression formation and recovery rates after heavy pumping cycles.
  - Eliminates human measurement errors and delivers real-time early warnings for drought and severe depletion.
